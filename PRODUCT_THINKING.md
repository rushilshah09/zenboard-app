# Zenboard — product thinking

**Status:** governing. Set by the user 2026-08-17. Sits above feature requests:
`DESIGN_CONSTITUTION.md` says what good looks like, `SPRINT_RULES.md` says how
work is sequenced, `CONSISTENCY_PRINCIPLE.md` says how far consistency goes, and
this says **what the product is for**.

> **Open Zenboard. Know what matters. Do the work.**

Not another project-management tool. The operating system for running a modern
creative business — for agencies, studios, freelancers and small teams.

## The test every feature must pass

Before building anything, answer all of these. A feature that cannot is not a
feature.

1. What real problem does this solve, and how often does the user hit it?
2. What do they do today, across how many tools, in how many clicks?
3. Can Zenboard remove those steps, or already know the answer?
4. Can they finish without leaving Zenboard?

**Do not add a feature because a competitor has it.**

## The three users

| user | the question they open with | what they need |
|---|---|---|
| **Founder** | "How is my business doing?" | overview, revenue, outstanding, capacity, client + project health, risks — *not* operational detail |
| **Employee** | "What do I need to do?" | today, priorities, deadlines, meetings, waiting items, context — without searching |
| **Client** | "What's happening, what do you need from me?" | progress, deliverables, approvals, files, next steps — and *nothing internal* |

## One connected system

Business → Clients → Projects → Tasks → People → Meetings → Files →
Communication → Approvals → Content → Time → Money.

**Information is never entered twice.** Creating a client makes it available to
projects, tasks, meetings, files, the portal, finance and reports at once.

## A task carries its context

A task is never an isolated checkbox. It holds its project, client, owner, due
date, brief, files, the client's feedback, the discussion, the current version
and its approval state. The user must never have to ask *where is the brief* or
*which file is latest*.

## Work that flows

- **Meetings become work:** meeting → notes → decisions → action items → tasks →
  project updates. Nobody rewrites a meeting into tasks by hand.
- **Content is first-class:** idea → brief → script → design → review → approval →
  scheduled → published. An agency's own content is not a second tool.
- **AI is invisible.** Not "ask Zenboard anything" — it extracts the action item,
  attaches the feedback to the right task, and notices the project at risk. AI
  operates the workspace; it is not another surface to manage.

## The seven final tests

**Founder:** business understood in 30 seconds. · **Employee:** today understood
in 10. · **Client:** status without asking. · **Context:** everything needed for
a task, without searching. · **Automation:** can Zenboard do part of it? ·
**Simplicity:** can a step be removed? · **Consistency:** does it behave like the
rest of Zenboard?

And the one that decides: **"Never go back"** — after 30 days, would returning to
the old workflow feel painful? If no, rethink the feature.

## The north star

Not features, screens or integrations. **How much work does Zenboard remove from
the user's day?**
