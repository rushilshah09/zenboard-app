# Zenboard launch film — illustration list

Eleven illustrations, all following the **Zenboard Illustration System** (deep burgundy `#280417` hand-drawn line, pink `#C41C72` as the single deliberate accent, restrained supporting tones, generous negative space).

They tell one small visual story of their own: **an idea (01) → too much at once (02) → a knot (03) → the same thread, straightened, connecting everything (04) → calm (05)**, plus six small "life" vignettes (06–11).

## How to deliver

| | |
|---|---|
| **Format** | SVG preferred (PNG 2048×2048 also works) |
| **File name** | exactly the name in the table, e.g. `founder-idea.svg` |
| **Where** | `launch-video/public/illustrations/` |
| **Canvas** | square 1:1 (`viewBox="0 0 2048 2048"` or any square) |
| **Background** | **transparent**: the film places each one on its own card colour (listed below). If your tool can't do transparent, use the listed card colour as the background fill. |
| **Text** | none |

As soon as a file lands in that folder, its numbered placeholder in the video is replaced by it. No code changes are needed.

### 01 · `founder-idea`

**Where:** Opening. The voice-over says *"Every business starts with one person… and a big idea."* It sits beside the words "…and a big idea."

> *[Master block]*
>
> **Subject:** One person, one big idea — the very beginning of a business.
>
> **Concept / action:** A lone person stands in the lower-left of the frame, stretching up on their toes with one arm raised, about to launch a small folded paper plane. A light, loose dotted flight path loops up and away towards the top-right, suggesting everything the idea could become. The person is small; the empty space ahead of them is large and full of possibility.
>
> **Important objects:** the person (simple, expressive, mid-motion), the paper plane (the only pink #C41C72 element), the dotted flight path in dusty rose #A94A72.
>
> **Composition:** figure in the lower-left third, plane at the tip of the raised hand, path sweeping diagonally to the top-right. At least half of the canvas is empty negative space. Transparent background.
>
> **Canvas:** 1:1

### 02 · `juggling`

**Where:** *"Then… the work shows up."* The video animates UI chips orbiting around this illustration, which sits inside a **circle**.

> *[Master block]*
>
> **Subject:** A founder the moment the work arrives, all at once.
>
> **Concept / action:** A person standing upright, arms raised, balancing a tall, precarious tower of mismatched work objects on their two open palms: a laptop, a calendar page, a speech bubble, a coffee cup and a receipt, stacked unevenly. The tower leans; the receipt at the top is just starting to tip off. Their face is calm but their eyebrows are slightly raised: coping, but stretched.
>
> **Important objects:** the person; the stacked tower (laptop, calendar page, speech bubble, coffee cup); the tipping receipt as the single pink #C41C72 accent.
>
> **Composition:** strictly centred and vertical, so everything fits inside a **circle** inscribed in the square (keep all important parts within the central circle, with nothing in the corners). Leave a clear ring of empty space around the figure, because animated elements orbit there. Transparent background.
>
> **Canvas:** 1:1

### 03 · `tangled-thread`

**Where:** *"By Friday, you've spent more time managing your work… than doing it."*

> *[Master block]*
>
> **Subject:** A working week that has turned into a knot.
>
> **Concept / action:** A huge ball of tangled thread, drawn in dense, restless loops, fills most of the frame. Caught inside the knot are tiny fragments of work: a checkbox, a calendar square, a dollar sign, a folded page. A small person sits at the bottom edge looking up at it, holding the one loose end of thread that trails out of the knot. That loose end is the way out.
>
> **Important objects:** the tangled knot (deep burgundy #280417 line, loops of varying weight); the tiny work fragments (plum #5B1839); the small seated person; the loose end in the person's hand as the only pink #C41C72 line.
>
> **Composition:** knot centred slightly above the middle, person small at the bottom-left, pink loose end connecting them. Transparent background.
>
> **Canvas:** 1:1

### 04 · `connected-thread`

**Where:** *"Because everything is connected."* This is the visual payoff of 03: the same thread, untangled.

> *[Master block]*
>
> **Subject:** Everything connected by one thread.

>
> **Composition:** thread runs across the middle with a slight upward curve, the hand entering from the left edge, objects evenly spaced, with generous space above and below. Transparent background.

## Consistency tips

- Generate **01** first and approve it, then use it as the style or reference image for every other illustration, so line weight and faces stay the same across all eleven.
- Keep one pink accent per illustration. If pink appears in more than one place, it stops meaning anything.
- 06–11 sit side by side in one grid: check them together at small size before exporting.
