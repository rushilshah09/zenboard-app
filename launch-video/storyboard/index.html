Launch film storyboard, v4

Eight scenes, 23 keyframes. It follows the review directions D1–D7: the juggling opener, the Flike-style resolve and funnel, the Berry product field, the split-screen modules, the plain pill wall, the 3D icon carousel and a calm ending.

How to review: reply with frame numbers, e.g. approve 1.1–2.3, change 4.1: darker field. These are layout keyframes. Final frames get real rendering, 3D lighting, depth of field and grain.
  • The man with the laptop is the person at the centre of scene 1.
  • Competitor apps are generic stand-in tiles (mail, chat, tasks, docs, CRM, calendar, invoices, notes). Swapping in real logos is still your call.
  • Scene 4 uses a Berry dark-to-light field. Each module could get its own field colour instead.
