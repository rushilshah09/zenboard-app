One motion language for the whole film

TravelEase in and out. Accelerates softly, decelerates naturally. No linear moves.
ArriveCritically damped spring. Lands without bounce.
HeroSpring with about 1.5% overshoot, only on the portrait, the Berry circle, the mark and the app icon.
SpinAngular velocity eases between speeds, so every spin builds momentum and coasts.
DepartExits accelerate away, faster than entrances.

Carried from the approved storyboard

  • Brand: Paper, Ink and one Berry accent; field colours only inside the UI
  • Geist everywhere, Geist Mono only for data; never full caps
  • Phosphor icons, filled; the real dashboard and feature cards
  • Real faces in the UI; the portrait at the centre of the orbit

What changed in this version

  • Rebuilt as one continuous animation instead of nine stitched scenes
  • Diagonal line from the top right opens the film and returns to align the mark
  • Circular motion runs from the orbit to the carousel to the mark
  • Logo reveal is earned: line, circles, alignment, mark, wordmark

Sound

  • Voice-over lands with its on-screen words
  • Ticks on each orbit launch and feature step; clicks on presses
  • Whoosh on travel, a chime when the mark forms
  • Score fades in over 1.5 s and out over the last 5 s
